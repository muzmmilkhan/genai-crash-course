Prompting Basics

Learn to ask clearly. Good prompts are the cheapest way to get better results from any GenAI tool.

What is a prompt?

A prompt is the message you give the AI. It can include instructions, background, examples, and the format you want. Think of it as briefing a smart new colleague who knows nothing about your situation.

Rule 1: Be specific

VagueSpecific
Write about our productWrite a 100-word announcement of our new budgeting app for college students. Friendly tone.

Rule 2: Give context and a role

Say who the answer is for and what situation you are in. A short role such as "You are a patient math tutor" changes tone and depth.

You are a patient math tutor for 12-year-olds.
Explain fractions using pizza examples. Keep it under 120 words.

Rule 3: Show examples

Showing 2-3 examples of what you want is often better than describing it. This is called few-shot prompting.

Rule 4: Ask for a format

  • Bullet list, table, steps, or JSON.
  • A length limit such as "under 80 words".
  • A tone such as "friendly" or "formal".

Rule 5: Let it think, then iterate

For tricky questions, ask for step-by-step reasoning. If the first answer is not right, reply with what to change instead of starting over.

Think through this step by step, then give the final answer on its own line.

Common mistakes

  • Asking for too many things at once.
  • Assuming the AI knows your context.
  • Not checking the facts in the answer.
  • Pasting private or confidential data into tools you are not approved to use.
