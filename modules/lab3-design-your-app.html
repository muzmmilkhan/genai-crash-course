Lab 3: Design Your Own GenAI App

Apply the architecture ideas: pick a product, choose its building blocks, then explain your choices.

Goal

Practise thinking like an architect. You will choose the pieces a GenAI product needs and justify each one.

  • No coding required.
  • About 25 minutes.

Step 1: Use the architecture builder

Step 2: Design your own idea

Think of a GenAI app you would like to exist. Write answers to these questions in a notes file:

  • Who is the user and what problem do they have?
  • What information does the model need? Is it in its training data, or must you retrieve it?
  • Does it need to take actions with tools? Which ones, and which need human approval?
  • What could go wrong? List two risks and one guardrail for each.
  • How will you test whether it works well?

Step 3: Estimate the cost

Reflection

  • Which block was hardest to decide on?
  • Where would a human need to stay in the loop?
